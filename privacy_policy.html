Privacy Policy - Stack Tower

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Stack Tower ("the Application"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy while you enjoy our tap-timing stacking game. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is processed when you download and play our game.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

The developer does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, address, contacts, or photos.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core gameplay features, Stack Tower stores minimal preferences directly on your local device storage:

  • High Score: Your personal best score achieved in the game.
  • Sound Settings: Your preference for sound effects (ON / OFF).

This data stays strictly on your device and is never uploaded to external servers.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stack Tower is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve contextual and personalized ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain device information, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device type, model, operating system version, and general hardware specifications
  • IP address (used to infer coarse location for ad relevance)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and viewability)

For more details on how Google handles advertising data and how you can opt out of personalized ads, please visit Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Stack Tower is designed for a general audience and is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

5. Security

We value your trust. While the app does not store personal data remotely, we utilize industry standard security practices and rely on Google's secure frameworks for advertising services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated date.
